Rules:

Player selects a number 1–6 and places a bet.

If correct: payoutMultiplier = 6.0, else 0.
Deduct bet once, then add payout if win.
UI:

Number selector (6 pill buttons).
Button “Rzuć kością”.
Simple dice animation: show a dice face that “shuffles” quickly for ~900–1200ms, then stops on final number.

Disable inputs during roll (isPlaying).

4) Mines game (/minigierki/mines)
Rules:

5x5 grid (25 tiles).
Player chooses mine count preset: 3, 6, or 12.
Player places a bet and starts a round (“Start”).
At round start generate mine positions ONCE and keep them fixed until round ends.
Each safe click increases a cashout multiplier.
Player can click “Cashout” anytime after at least 1 safe pick.
If player hits a mine: round ends, bet is lost.
Multiplier (simple, deterministic, no complex math):

Use a small predefined multiplier table based on safe picks and mine count OR a simple formula.
Keep it reasonable and increasing; show current multiplier clearly.
Example simple approach:
baseIncrement depends on mines:
3 mines: +0.20 per safe pick
6 mines: +0.35 per safe pick
12 mines: +0.60 per safe pick
multiplier starts at 1.0 and increases by increment per safe pick (cap optional).
(This is not “casino-accurate” but fine for a sim.)
Payout:

On cashout: payout = bet * multiplier.
Deduct bet once at start of round.
On mine hit: payout 0.
UI:

Mine count selector: 3 / 6 / 12 (pill buttons).
BetPanel + “Start”
Grid of tiles (glass cards). Clicking reveals:
safe: show a gem/check icon
mine: show bomb icon + end animation (subtle)
Show:
“Bezpieczne trafienia: N”
“Mnożnik: xM”
“Potencjalna wypłata: #X.XX”
Buttons:
“Cashout” (enabled only if safe picks >= 1 and round active)
“Nowa runda” after end
Guards:

While a round is active, prevent changing mine count and bet amount.
Prevent double cashout.
Stats:

Each started round counts as one minigame played and wagered += bet.
Profit = payout - bet (net), or -bet if busted.
5) Acceptance
Both games work without negative balance and without double-execution bugs.
Mines minefield is generated once per round and consistent.
Stats are updated correctly.
Animations are smooth but not invasive.
